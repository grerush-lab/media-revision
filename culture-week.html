Sounds & stories

Music, radio and TV carry culture too. Quick reminders, then build your own Culture Week castaway card.

Music video · 2019

Lizzo, Good as Hell

A Black woman with a body type rarely shown in pop videos, celebrated with pride. Women support and rescue each other.

Who usually gets to be the star of a pop video?

Music video · 2020

Bieber ft. Quavo, Intentions

Made with Alexandria House, an LA charity helping women and children out of homelessness. Women of colour are shown as active and ambitious.

Empowering, or a “white saviour” story? You decide.

Music video · 1995

TLC, Waterfalls

Three Black women artists tell hard stories about life in 1990s America: crime, risk and HIV/AIDS. Pop with a social message.

How does it reflect the time it was made?

Radio · since 1942

Desert Island Discs

A guest picks 8 records, 1 book and 1 luxury to tell their life story. Guests come from every nation, region and background.

What would your choices say about your identity?

TV crime drama · 2024

Trigger Point

A modern London bomb squad: Hass Rahim leads the unit while Lana is away, with Danny alongside them. Diversity is shown as normal, not as a storyline.

Compare with The Sweeney (1975): who was in charge then?

    A Level set texts through a cultural lens

    Year 12–13: these all link straight to Culture Week. GCSE students: a preview of what’s next.

    Music video · 2016

    Beyoncé, Formation

    Black Southern identity and heritage, set against New Orleans after Hurricane Katrina and the rise of Black Lives Matter.

    How does it celebrate culture and protest at the same time?

    Film industry · 2018

    Black Panther

    A Black-led Marvel blockbuster built on Afrofuturism: an African nation shown as powerful and technologically advanced. Earned over $1.3 billion worldwide.

    Why did Disney back it, and what did its success prove?

    Video game · 2012

    Assassin’s Creed III: Liberation

    Aveline, a woman of French and African heritage, is the lead in 1760s New Orleans, in a story that deals with slavery.

    Who usually gets to be the hero of a game?

    Online magazine

    Thiiird

    An independent magazine by and for Black British creatives, made outside the big media companies.

    What can independent media say that mainstream media won’t?

    TV · Scandinavian noir

    The Bridge

    A Swedish–Danish crime drama, subtitled and shown on BBC Four: global TV that British audiences chose to watch.

    Why do audiences enjoy stories from other cultures?

Revision cards

Passive audience

The audience absorbs the media message without questioning it. Linked to the hypodermic needle idea: the message is "injected" straight in.

Active audience

The audience interprets, questions, chooses and interacts. They might share, comment, play, make playlists, guess the plot or argue about meaning. Links to uses and gratifications.

Young & Rubicam's 4Cs

  • Mainstreamers want security and to belong; they buy familiar brands.
  • Explorers want discovery and new experiences.
  • Aspirers want status, image and fame.
  • Succeeders want control; they have already "made it".
  • Reformers want to improve society; they value knowledge and ethics.

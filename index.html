Component 1 — Exploring the Media 1hr 30min · 80 marks · 40%
Section A — Media Language & Representation · 45 marks
Q1 · 15 marks Media Language

Analyse one print set text — layout, images, language, genre conventions.

No introduction needed. Get straight into analysis. Each point needs a clear connotation, not just a description. Reference to contexts may be required.

Film posters · Magazines · Adverts · Newspapers

Q2(a) · 5 marks Context

How does a context (historical / social / political / cultural) influence the set product?

Spend 5 minutes max. Give brief textual examples to support each point. Uses a different media form from Q1.

Always a different product from Q1 — e.g. if Q1 is a film poster, Q2 will be a magazine or newspaper front cover.

Q2(b) · 25 marks Representation

Compare a set text with an unseen resource. Extended response — needs introduction, sustained analysis, and conclusion.

Spend 10 minutes studying the unseen resource before writing. Both texts must be covered. The unseen is the same media form as the set text.

The unseen resource is provided in the exam. Apply representation theory — selection, construction, mediation, stereotypes, messages and values.

Section B — Industries & Audiences · 35 marks
Q3 · stepped Industries
Q4 · stepped Audiences

Stepped questions — short low-mark parts leading into a 10–12 mark extended question.

Q3 and Q4 always cover different media forms. 1 mark = 1 minute of writing. Read all Section B questions together before starting — some parts relate to each other.

No Time to Die · Fortnite · The Sun · The Archers

Component 2 — Understanding Media Forms and Products 1hr 30min · 60 marks · 30%
Section A — Television · 30 marks
Q1(a) · 8–10 marks ML or Representation

Analyse the extract — media language or representation.

The extract (~3 mins) is shown twice with a 6-minute note-taking break. Make detailed notes both times. You have 2 minutes to read the questions before the extract is shown — use this to know what to look for.

Q1(b) · 10–12 marks Judgements

Analyse and make judgements and conclusions about the same extract. 'How far' questions need a clear line of argument and a conclusion.

Luther (2010) + The Sweeney (1975) — Crime Drama option

Q2 · 10 marks Industries / Audiences / Contexts

Knowledge and understanding of your set TV shows — no extract needed here.

Could be about the BBC's PSB remit, how technology has changed TV consumption, social or cultural contexts, or audience theory. Refer directly to Luther and/or The Sweeney.

Whichever of Industries / Audiences / Contexts appears in Q2 will not appear in Q4. They are always different.

Section B — Music · 30 marks
Q3 · 20 marks ML or Representation

Extended essay on both contemporary music videos. No extract is shown — this relies entirely on revision.

Whichever area (ML or Representation) wasn't assessed in Q1 will appear here. Both set videos must be covered — if you only write about one, marks are halved. Needs intro, sustained discussion, conclusion.

Lizzo / Taylor Swift + Bieber / Stormzy + TLC / Duran Duran

Q4 · 10 marks Industries / Audiences / Contexts

Knowledge of the music industry, how audiences use artist websites, or how music videos reflect their time.

Refer to the artist websites and/or the historical music video (TLC Waterfalls or Duran Duran Rio). Whichever topic wasn't used in Q2 will appear here.

Artist websites (Lizzo, Bieber etc.) are key here — study their homepages, how they promote music, links to social media, commercial features.

Key patterns to remember
In C1, Q1 and Q2 always assess different media forms — e.g. if Q1 is a film poster, Q2 will be a magazine or newspaper.
In C1, Q3 and Q4 also always assess different media forms from each other.
In C2, Q1 and Q3 are always the same area (both Media Language, or both Representation) — whichever Q1 uses, Q3 uses the same.
In C2, Q2 and Q4 are always different topics — if Q2 is Industries, Q4 will be Audiences or Contexts, never Industries again.
C2 Q3 is always 20 marks and always an extended essay — no extract is shown. You must know your music videos and websites from memory.
The unseen resource in C1 Q2(b) is always the same media form as the set text (e.g. both newspapers, both magazines). Spend 10 minutes analysing it before you write.

Component 1 — Section A · Media Language & Representation · 45 marks
Set texts — print media forms
GQ Aug 2019 Vogue Jul 2021 Golden Gun poster No Time to Die poster The Guardian Jan 2022 The Sun Jan 2021 Quality Street 1956 NHS advert
Q1 · 15m Media Language

Analyse one print set text — layout, images, language, genre conventions. 2–3 sub-questions.

► Example question
Example — 2025 style

Explore how the print advertisement for Quality Street uses media language to communicate meanings:

(a) layout and design [5]

(b) images [10]

Q2(a) · 5m Context

How does a context (historical / social / political / cultural) influence the set product? Different set text from Q1.

► Example question
Example — 2025 style

Explain how social and cultural contexts influence magazines. Refer to the Vogue front cover to support your points. [5]

Q2(b) · 25m Representation

Compare set text with an unseen resource (same media form). Extended response — unseen provided in exam.

► Example question
Example — 2025 style

Compare the representation of ethnicity in the Vogue and Grazia front covers. [25]

In your answer, you must consider:

  • the choices the producers have made about how to represent ethnicity
  • how far the representation of ethnicity is similar in the two covers
  • how far the representation of ethnicity is different in the two covers
Component 1 — Section B · Industries & Audiences · 35 marks
Set texts
No Time to Die + 007.com Fortnite + epicgames.com The Sun + thesun.co.uk The Archers + BBC Radio 4
Q3 · stepped Industries

Short 1–2m parts building to a 10–12m extended question. Regulation, funding, distribution, promotion.

► Example question
Example — 2025 style

(a) Name the organisation that regulates film in the UK. [1]

(b) Briefly explain the advantages of vertical integration for the film industry. [4]

(c) Explain how the film industry uses convergence to promote films. Refer to the 007 website to support your points. [12]

Q4 · stepped Audiences

Short 1–2m parts building to a 10–12m extended question. Targeting, engagement, Uses & Gratifications.

► Example question
Example — 2025 style

(a) Identify two different audiences for Fortnite. [2]

(b) Briefly explain how video games target audiences through marketing. Refer to Fortnite to support your points. [4]

(c) Explain how audiences interact with video games. Refer to the Uses and Gratifications theory and Fortnite to support your points. [12]

“How far” question on the same extract — needs a clear argument and conclusion.

Knowledge of your set shows — no extract needed.

► Example question

How do crime dramas reflect their historical contexts? Refer to Trigger Point or The Sweeney to support your points. [10]

Component 2 — Section B · Music · 30 marks
Set texts — no extract shown, relies on revision
Lizzo — Good as Hell OR Taylor Swift — The Man Stormzy — Superheroes OR Justin Bieber — Intentions + artist websites
Historical: Duran Duran — Rio (1982) OR TLC — Waterfalls (1995)
Q3 · 20m Media Language or Representation

Extended essay on both contemporary music videos. Must cover both — marks halved if only one covered.

► Example question
Example — 2025 style

How far do music videos represent social issues? [20]

In your response, you must:

  • explore representations of social issues in the two music videos you have studied
  • refer to relevant media contexts
  • make judgements and draw conclusions
Q4 · 10m Contexts / Industry / Audience

Knowledge of the music industry, websites, or how videos reflect their time. Whichever topic wasn’t in Q2.

► Example question
Example — 2025 style

Explain how users interact with music websites. Refer to the official Lizzo website or the official Justin Bieber website to support your response. [10]

→C2 Q3 is always 20 marks — no extract shown
→C1 Q2(b) unseen is always the same media form as the set text
